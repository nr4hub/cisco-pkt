🇱🇰 Sinhala Vocabulary — දුෂ්කර වචන

Key English words from this guide, with Sinhala meanings and example sentences.

topologynoun
🇬🇧 English

The layout showing how devices in a network are connected.

🇱🇰 සිංහල

ජාලයේ උපාංග සම්බන්ධ වී ඇති සැලැස්ම හෝ හැඩය

Sample Sentences
  • DailyOur school's bus routes form a star topology around the town centre.
  • NetA star topology connects every PC to one central switch.
  • SoftwareDraw the topology in Packet Tracer before you configure anything.
protocolnoun
🇬🇧 English

An agreed set of rules that devices follow to communicate.

🇱🇰 සිංහල

උපාංග අතර සන්නිවේදනය සඳහා පිළිගත් නීති සමූහයක්

Sample Sentences
  • DailyVisitors must follow the school's security protocol at the gate.
  • NetHTTP and DNS are application-layer protocols.
  • CyberTelnet is a weak protocol because it sends passwords in plain text.
encapsulationnoun
🇬🇧 English

Wrapping data with a header (and trailer) at each layer.

🇱🇰 සිංහල

දත්ත වටා header එකක් එකතු කර ඇසුරුම් කිරීම

Sample Sentences
  • DailyPutting a letter in an envelope and then in a parcel is like encapsulation.
  • NetA segment becomes a packet, then a frame, through encapsulation.
  • SoftwareSimulation mode lets you inspect each layer added during encapsulation.
broadcastnoun
🇬🇧 English

A message sent to every device in the same network.

🇱🇰 සිංහල

ජාලයේ සිටින සියලු උපාංගවලට එකවර යවන පණිවිඩයක්

Sample Sentences
  • DailyThe principal made a broadcast over the school speakers.
  • NetA DHCP Discover is a broadcast to 255.255.255.255.
  • CyberToo many broadcast packets can slow down or flood a network.
gatewaynoun
🇬🇧 English

The router address a device uses to reach other networks.

🇱🇰 සිංහල

එක් ජාලයක සිට වෙනත් ජාලයකට යාමට ඇති දොරටුව (router ලිපිනය)

Sample Sentences
  • DailyThe main gateway is the only way into the campus.
  • NetSet the PC's default gateway to 192.168.1.1.
  • SoftwareWithout a gateway, your ping to another network will fail.
subnetnoun
🇬🇧 English

A smaller network created by dividing a larger network.

🇱🇰 සිංහල

විශාල ජාලයක් බෙදා සාදන කුඩා ජාලයක්

Sample Sentences
  • DailyEach floor of the office has its own subnet.
  • Net192.168.1.0/26 is a subnet of 192.168.1.0/24.
  • CyberPlacing servers in a separate subnet limits how far an attacker can spread.
leasenoun
🇬🇧 English

The time a device may keep an address given to it.

🇱🇰 සිංහල

ලිපිනයක් තාවකාලිකව භාවිතයට ලැබෙන කාලය

Sample Sentences
  • DailyWe signed a one-year lease on the apartment.
  • NetA DHCP lease of 7 days means the PC renews its address weekly.
  • SoftwareUse ipconfig /renew to ask for a new lease.
bandwidthnoun
🇬🇧 English

The maximum amount of data that can be sent per second.

🇱🇰 සිංහල

තත්පරයකදී යැවිය හැකි උපරිම දත්ත ප්‍රමාණය

Sample Sentences
  • DailyVideo calls need more bandwidth than text chats.
  • NetOSPF cost is calculated from interface bandwidth.
  • HardwareA Gigabit port has ten times the bandwidth of a Fast Ethernet port.
trunknoun
🇬🇧 English

A link that carries traffic for several VLANs at once.

🇱🇰 සිංහල

VLAN කිහිපයක් එකවර රැගෙන යන සම්බන්ධතාවය

Sample Sentences
  • DailyThe trunk road carries buses from many villages.
  • NetA trunk port tags frames with 802.1Q so VLANs stay separate.
  • HardwareConnect two switches with a trunk on their Gigabit ports.
redundancynoun
🇬🇧 English

Having a spare path or device in case one fails.

🇱🇰 සිංහල

එකක් අසාර්ථක වුවහොත් ආදේශ වීමට අමතර විකල්පයක් තිබීම

Sample Sentences
  • DailyThe hospital has a backup generator for redundancy.
  • NetA floating static route gives redundancy for a failed link.
  • CyberGood redundancy helps a service survive an attack or outage.
convergencenoun
🇬🇧 English

When all routers agree on the same view of the network.

🇱🇰 සිංහල

සියලු routers එකම ජාල තොරතුරු වලට එකඟ වීමේ තත්වය

Sample Sentences
  • DailyAfter a long meeting the team reached convergence on one plan.
  • NetOSPF convergence is fast after a link goes down.
  • SoftwareWait for convergence before you test your pings.
translationnoun
🇬🇧 English

Changing something from one form into another.

🇱🇰 සිංහල

එක් ආකාරයකින් තවත් ආකාරයකට පරිවර්තනය කිරීම

Sample Sentences
  • DailyThe Sinhala translation helps new learners follow the lesson.
  • NetNAT translation maps private addresses to a public address.
  • SoftwareCheck the translation table with show ip nat translations.
